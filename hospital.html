What this is

A diagnostic bay. Any model in this repository can be admitted — a built world with a full chart, or a bare mesh in STL, Collada or glTF — and the same instruments run on it. Nothing here needs a key or a network.

Every study is calibrated before it is believed: the ray scanner is fired at a box known to be sealed, and at the same box with a wall taken off. If the sealed box leaks, the instrument is broken and nothing it says about a building means anything. That is the only reading here that can tell the building is wrong from our eyes are wrong.
