Applications

Operative Builder

A Three.js building environment that answers back. Instructions become framing operations; the framing reports what the instruction did to it — cut studs, over-sized bores, spans, missing bearing, a roof that no longer meets its walls — and every member can say how it became what it is.

Gunnar Thunder Builder

The new mobile-first builder base. Import one DAE/STL as the foundation, then build with widgets, layers, and scene hierarchy controls.
